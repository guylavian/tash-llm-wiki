## Answer (community) — Microsoft Moderator [MicrosoftEmployee]

*upvotes: 0 · updated: 2021-11-29*

The variables in 500 and 501 are just dumping the what is evaluated in your claim issuance and authorization rules (so it the content will depend on your rules).  

The foolowing site is listing all possible ADFS events: https://adfshelp.microsoft.com/AdfsEventViewer/GetAdfsEventList  

I also put that together ealier today to better understand what events are generated depending on the sign-in context and results: https://github.com/CanadianShield/ADFSLogs
