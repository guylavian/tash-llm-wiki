Thanks for your description! It seems that this is an issue related to alias. Do you have any non-Microsoft domain(like orange, Gmail) alias for your Microsoft account? I'd really like you to check your alias, just in case.

Go to this website to manage your alias, see if you can find something else: Manage how you sign in to Microsoft

Here is the thing, from my experience, you need to set your @outlook.com email address as your primary alias, just click the "Make primary" on the right side of your Outlook email address.

If you don't have any @outlook.com alias in your Microsoft account, select "Create a new email address and add it as an alias" in Add an alias.

I will just tell you how I encountered this issue for your reference:

I encountered this issue when I have a Gmail as my alias and I set Gmail address as my primary account, then I add an Outlook account as my alias, when I log in this Outlook email address, I get this random email address just like you said, I fixed this by removing the Gmail account from my alias and setting the Outlook email address as my primary alias.
