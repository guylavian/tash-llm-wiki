Dear Cheri, 

Thank you for contacting the Microsoft Support Community. We understand how frustrating it can be to suddenly encounter these errors after years of using your Bigpond email in Outlook. Error code 0x800CCC69 is often related to an SMTP issue, meaning your outgoing mail settings may need adjusting. Here are some steps to help resolve the issue: 

-  Verify SMTP Settings – Incorrect outgoing mail server settings can cause problems. Double-check that your settings match Bigpond's recommended configuration.

-  Restart Your Computer & Router – A simple restart can often clear temporary glitches affecting mail delivery

-  Repair Outlook Installation – If the issue persists, repairing your Outlook installation might resolve any underlying system file corruption - Repair an Office application - Microsoft Support.

-  Reset Outlook Settings – Sometimes, resetting Outlook to its default settings can fix stubborn errors.

-  Check Account Access Permissions – Ensure your Bigpond account has full access rights to configure email settings.
