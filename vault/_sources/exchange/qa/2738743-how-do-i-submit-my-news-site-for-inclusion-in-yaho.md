Hi,

All press releases on Yahoo originate from other sources. We can't take individual press releases and add them to our service.

-  To have your corporate press releases submitted to Yahoo, please contact PR Newswire, Business Wire, GlobeNewswire, or Market Wired.

-  For Canadian corporate press releases, please contact Market Wired or Canada Newswire.

-  For government, political, or non-profit press releases, please contact PR Newswire.

NOTE: For a fee, these services will make sure hundreds of news services are aware of your announcements.
