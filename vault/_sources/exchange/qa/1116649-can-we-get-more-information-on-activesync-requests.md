## Question

My organisation is moving its mobile devices across to EndPoint (mostly iPads and Android phones) but are having issues with identifying devices in the ActiveSync on Exchange 2013. We are experiencing an issue with identifying whether an ActiveSync request has come from one of our own devices, or a personal device that is also using the Outlook app. We do not authorise our users to access their emails on personal devices for data security reasons, which is why we need to be able to confirm what devices are which.    

Is there any way we can differentiate these devices? I find it odd that Microsoft's own application and infrastructure are so limited in terms of information they share as part of an ActiveSync request. This wouldn't be so much of an issue for us if we only had to do this once per device, but every time the app is updated we have to go through another round of approvals, that is bloating the listed devices against all of our mailboxes and making it even more difficult to find the actual device we need to approve.
