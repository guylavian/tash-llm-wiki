# Can I use Microsoft Exchange 2019 SAL licenses and CAL licenses in the same Exchange environment?

## Question

Basically, can i have a mix of our company's purchased perpetual volume licenses (not used previously) and for another list of users use the subscription based from a SPLA provider?

this is for the same Exchange environment/deployment.

Yes, you can mix perpetual volume licenses with subscription-based licenses from a Service Provider License Agreement (SPLA) provider in the same Exchange environment. However, there are a few key considerations:

-  Ensure that all users and devices accessing your Exchange environment are properly licensed according to the terms and conditions of both the perpetual volume licenses and the SPLA licenses.

-  Track which users are covered by perpetual licenses and which are covered by SPLA licenses to ensure compliance in case of audits.

-  Ensure that the version of Exchange Server you use is supported under both licensing schemes.

-  Verify that the mixed-use licensing model does not cause any technical or support issues in your Exchange environment.

-  Assign perpetual licenses to specific user mailboxes and ensure that the rest of the users are covered by SPLA licenses.
