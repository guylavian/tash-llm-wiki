## Question

MSN is my home page for IE and I love some of the articles, news and information about different surveys that they run on best colleges, best cities to live in, etc. But of late I am seeing so much of negative news and depressing crime beat related news. 

So much so that I want to avoid even looking at it when I open a new tab in the browser.

This is the time for media to uplift moods and help change the tide. 

Media can play a pivotal role in helping change mind sets and raise hope. A constant barrage of negative news does not help to create hope or change lives, but in fact does the exact opposite. So many times leaders are trying to do good for their people but this information does not reach the public because the media chooses to focus only on the negative. That being said, is there also any way I can personalize my home page?

At www.msn.com you can't block a specific news source or type of story. The 

Windows 10 News App and https://microsoftnews.msn.com/ give you the ability to block 

a source and other customization options that msn.com used to provide.

Some info here

https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/topic/microsoft-news-feedback-frequently-asked-questions-efc5ae67-74c0-4a5a-dfbe-20db14c8255f

but, basically to block a source you click on the 3 dots on the blurb, the image and summary that would take you to the story and one of the options will be to block the source.

You can submit feedback via the Feedback link at the bottom of nearly every MSN 

webpage.
