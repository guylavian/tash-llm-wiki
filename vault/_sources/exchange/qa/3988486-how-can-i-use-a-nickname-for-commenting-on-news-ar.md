## Question

Hi

I have noticed that when I am commenting on a news article it displays my full name.

When I go into 'edit' in the profile page on my microsoft account it has a name that i would use. If I change my name associated with the email then that affects other aspects of what I do.

Are there ways around of getting around this?

Thanks

## Answer (community) — community member

Hi, Debbie V1 

Welcome to Microsoft Community.

We understand that you would like to use a nickname to post comments on the MSN news site. 
It should be explained that the account information used on MSN and Microsoft Account is common, so if you change your name or nickname, it will be synchronized on all Microsoft-related websites, and Microsoft has not yet set up a separate MSN news nickname comment function. As a user of the Microsoft website, I do comment during the week, but due to restrictions I would change to my own private account, so I agree with your idea.
As an alternative, you could try the option of creating a new account for news browsing and segregating it from your work-specific account:
Please use your free email address to sign up for a dedicated account to browse and comment on the news, so that you can post comments under your own nickname and not worry about work-related issues getting in the way. After you sign up for a new account, you can click on your avatar box in the top left corner of Edge and create a new Profile and log in to the account you're using for commenting, and then you can use that Profile to browse the news and post comments.
And regarding your proposal to use nicknames to comment on news articles, you may submit feedback in the Microsoft Edge browser as well. Open the page in the Microsoft Edge browser, then use the shortcut keys "Alt" + "Shift" + "I" to open the feedback window or select the 3 dots in the top right, select Help and feedback, then choose Send feedback (Note that the feedback window will capture the current screen and URL link and email address, and you can add Attachment and record your problem), enter your problem, and click "Send".

Thank you for your understanding and support, if you have any questions, you are welcome to contact us at any time!
