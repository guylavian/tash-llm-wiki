## Question

Aside from the obvious – restricting who can access the exchange admin centre and what roles/permissions they have once logged into the exchange admin centre – are there any ‘extra’ security controls that are recommended to help reduce as much as possible the likelihood of someone gaining unauthorized access to the exchange admin centre.
I know for ‘external’ access to an exchange online user mailbox there are complex passwords + MFA safeguards to reduce the likelihood of those individual accounts being compromised, but I just wondered if there was anything extra that should be done to help protect against breaches of the exchange admin centre itself (or accounts with access to), as breach of that level is clearly higher impact that just a single account/mailbox.
