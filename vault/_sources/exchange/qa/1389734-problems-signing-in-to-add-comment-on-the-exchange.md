## Question

Hello,

I am trying to ask a question/comment on the Exchange Team blog page: https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/t5/exchange-team-blog/released-october-2023-exchange-server-security-updates/ba-p/3950647 

It requires singing in, however, after I enter my username and password it never logs me in and provides no error message. It does not show me as logged in.  I have updated Chrome and Edge and deleted the cache but am still unable to log in.

It states the fix is in CVE-2023-36434.  This is in the monthly update in KB5031362.  in the knowledge article for KB5031362 - https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/topic/october-10-2023-kb5031362-os-build-14393-6351-0c6e713e-3d6a-4593-8a75-af0a605f249c  it states that the latest servicing stack update KB5031467 should be installed first.

However, there is no KB5031467 update in the microsoft update catalog.  The latest SSU was made available in September- KB5030504. 

I believe it is a typo stating that KB5031467 is needed but just wanted to confirm this.
