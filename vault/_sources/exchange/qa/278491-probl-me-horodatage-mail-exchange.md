Here are several questions:    

-  Did you actually receive the email on 03/10/2016 instead of 03/12/2016?    

-  And since the email was sent and received five years ago, do you have other recent emails with the same problem?    

If there are any, please provide some examples with screenshots or in text if possible.    

(Please remember to hide your personal information for security)    

And would you please also check the date in message header via Outlook?    

Here are the steps:    

-  open the email    

-  locate File>Properties    

-  check Date information(under the From,To,CC,Subject part) in the Internet headers
