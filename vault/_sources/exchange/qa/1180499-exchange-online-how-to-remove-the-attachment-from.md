## Question

People are sending mails to our tenant with malware in the attachment. The recipient is no longer there. An NDR with the same attachment is being sent to the sender.

How can I remove the attachment from the NDR in Exchange Online.

## Answer (community) — Q&A User

*upvotes: 0 · updated: 2023-02-16*

Hello @Anonymous  

Unfortunately, by Mail Server design, this is not possible. (not only exchange server)

Attachment cannot be modified or removed by the sender or the recipient of the NDR, as it is automatically generated by the email server and is an integral part of the NDR message concept.
