## Question

Hello,

normally the Group Owner is the Department Manager and I want a new Manager to get the emails when the Admin changes the Ownership field in Exchange Online. How to send email to Group Owner?  

I know that if an email is sent to the Group Name@domain, all members will receive a copy, but how can I send it only to the Group Owner?

## Answer (community) — community member

*upvotes: 0 · updated: 2023-02-09*

Hi @Abuenon ,

There is no way to send an email to just the group owner, you could send him a separate email to notify him of his appointment as group owner.
