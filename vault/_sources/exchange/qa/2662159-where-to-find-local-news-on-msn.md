## Question

They've moved the Local link that used to be on the main page News module to the secondary News page.

To find Local news, click on "News" on the top horizontal menu:

You can also click on "News" in the "News" module; both of these links will take you to this secondary news page:

Clicking on "Local" here will take you to the tertiary Local news page, which for me in Los Angeles contains both local newspapers, a couple of television stations, and the LA Weekly.

At the top left of this tertiary Local page there is a drop down menu where you can change the location. It also seems to keep several recent locations for you to choose from so you can easily revisit them, like so:

I know some of you have been told to add the URL of your local newspaper to the My Topics page. This doesn't work. The My Topics list takes any URL added to it, chews it up, thoroughly mangles it, and spits it out as a bunch of useless garbage. Sure, you
 get a selection of stories, but almost none of them are from the publication you requested, and almost all of them are irrelevant and/or outdated.
