## Question

I got the update notification for my pc, and it looked normal. I run frequent virus scans on a couple separate antivirus programs, like the built in windows antivirus and Malwarebytes. I scan downloads and do everything I can to keep my computer healthy. So I installed the updates, and upon restarting my PC, everything was normal, until I noticed the news bar. Hey there!

My name is Ravi, and I am an independent advisor. I am happy to help you. :)

I understand how concerning it can be to see Hindi or a different language in the news feed. 

I have come across this issue in the past. It can happen after a certain update. It can also happen when a computer's location is different or its settings aren't applied. 

Please check the news and feed language.

1). Click on the News and Interests icon on the taskbar.

2). Click on the Settings (3 dots) button at the top right corner of News and Interests, and click on Language and content. 

3). On the Experience Settings > Language & content settings page in Microsoft Edge, select the language

If it's already English, try selecting a different language, saving it, and then selecting English again. 

You can check this tutorial: https://www.tenforums.com/tutorials/179206-how-...
