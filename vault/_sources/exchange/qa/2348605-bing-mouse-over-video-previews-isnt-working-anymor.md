I am happy to help you.

Check your Browser Extensions,Extensions can sometimes interfere with website functionality. Try disabling them to see if it resolves the issue.

```
1.	Open Microsoft Edge.

2.	Click on the three-dot menu in the upper-right corner.

3.	Go to Extensions.

4.	Toggle off all extensions or remove any that might be interfering.
```

Another thing try to Clear Browser Cache

Clearing the cache can resolve various issues related to website functionality.

```
1.	Open Microsoft Edge.

2.	Click on the three-dot menu in the upper-right corner.

3.	Go to Settings > Privacy, search, and services.

4.	Under Clear browsing data, click Choose what to clear.

5.	Select Cached images and files and click Clear now.
```
