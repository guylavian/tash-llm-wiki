## Question

Hey,

IT guy here. Recently I encountered a request of one of my users and it's linked to the news widget of Windows 11. The user got a new PC and changed from Win 10 to Win 11. Nothing big, right? Everything is setup and the usual procedure is followed but the person actually uses the news app for their job. The region of the PC is England and the language is in English but he needs the news in, lets say, Japanese. However the only way to achieve this is to set the region to Japan which isn't even remotely correct.

Now, how can I change the language for only the news without making such drastic changes? Why is it possible in Windows 10 but not 11? Isn't a news app supposed to show the news that interest the user?

So please tell me. Is there another way to change the language, similar to the Windows 10 version (Click on user icon > select from drop down list), that is easy for any none IT guy and only takes 3-5 clicks? Also if you know, why did Microsoft decide to make their newer product actually worse?

We checked for you and found that the display content of these widgets is indeed related to the Windows display language you mentioned, and there seems to be no other modification options. 

It may be that the Microsoft team believes that users who like to read news information in other languages ​​in their local area account for a relatively small proportion, so some of these settings have been modified. 

We suggest that you report this situation to the Microsoft team, and the problems caused by the design need to be updated and solved by the relevant team.
