# where do i find my exchange password?

## Question

I am required to fill in my MS Exchange password. The one I have doesn't work. How do I change it?

## Answer (community) — Microsoft Moderator [MicrosoftEmployee]

Hi Bert Reul

Thanks for reaching out to Microsoft Q&A.

If you are using a Outlook.com or a hotmail.com or msn.com domain you need to sign in first on the Outlook website to ensure that you have the exact password (link below)

https://outlook.live.com 

Here's how to update your password from the Outlook app

Open the Outlook App:

   Launch the Outlook app on your device.

   Go to Settings:

```
Tap on your profile icon or the gear icon (usually found at the top-left or bottom-right corner, depending on your device).
  
  **Access Account Settings:**
  
     Scroll down to find "Accounts" or "Manage Accounts."
     
        Select the email account for which you want to update the password.
        
        **Update Password:**
        
           Look for the password field or an option like "Change account settings."
           
              Enter your new password and save the changes.
              
              **Test the Update:**
              
                 Once you've updated the password, ensure your email account syncs properly by sending or receiving a test email.
```

Other way to update password:

-  Open the Control Panel:

-  On your Windows computer, type "Control Panel" in the search bar and open it.

-  Navigate to Mail Settings:

-  In the Control Panel, look for "Mail" (this might be listed as "Mail (Microsoft Outlook)" if installed).

-  Click on it to open the Mail Setup window.

-  Access Account Settings:

-  In the Mail Setup window, click on "Email Accounts."

-  This will open the Account Settings window.

-  Select Your Email Account:

-  Under the "Email" tab, find the email account you want to update.

-  Highlight it and click on "Change."

-  Update Your Password:

-  In the next window, look for the password field. Enter your updated password.

-  Save the changes and close all windows.

-  Restart Outlook:
