## Question

## Answer (community) — community member

*upvotes: 0 · updated: 2024-10-22*

Hello Darunee,

Welcome to Microsoft Community.

If you read everything in the link, you should know that Advanced Exchange for Surface is aimed at business devices. 

For individual users, the usual situation is that within a period of time after receiving a new product, if there is a quality problem with the device, you can contact the Microsoft Store support staff for a return or exchange. In such cases, Microsoft sends a new device to the customer. The warranty for each Surface device is calculated separately. 

However, I am concerned that this does not apply to business devices, so to avoid sending you the wrong message, I recommend that you contact Surface Support for business on this issue, they can provide you with the most accurate information:

Contact Surface Support for Business and Education customers - Surface | Microsoft Learn
