## Question

When I move eMails from my Exchange eMail account in Outlook 365 to the Archive, they are not archived on Outlook App on iPhone 11 and are still in the Inbox. What can I do?

## Answer (community) — community member

*upvotes: 0 · updated: 2020-10-27*

Please check your mailbox in OWA to see if  the message does exist in Archived folder.    

If yes, tap Setting on your phone and find your account, tap "Reset Account" then wait for some time and check the Archived folder on your phone.    

If still not working, try clearing the cache for Outlook app in setting (it seems IOS cannot do this?), or re-installing Outlook app.    

Apart from the archived sync issue, have you met other problems with Outlook on your phone?
