Hi atnc,

I just want to verify are you using an email client like Windows Live Mail to check for your emails or you are using a web browser like Google Chrome, Firefox etc? Please be informed that there are some instances that the user
 is not receiving emails due to filter settings of the account. For this reason, I suggest that you check your blocked senders list and then add your contacts to your safe senders list and access account using a web browser. Here are the steps:

Check your Blocked sender list1. Sign in to your account. Go to inbox.  

-  Click Options located at the upper-right side of the page.

-  Select More Options.   

-  Under Preventing junk email, click Safe and blocked senders.  

-  Click Blocked senders.  

-  In the Blocked senders list, select the name or domain (the part of an e-mail address that follows the @ sign, such as hotmail.com) that you now want to receive mail from, and then click
<< Remove from list.

Add the email address to your Safe senders list1. Sign in to your account. Go to inbox.  

-  Click Options located at the upper-right side of the page.

-  Select More Options.   

-  Under Preventing junk email, click Safe and blocked senders.  

-  Click Safe Senders.   

-  In the text box, type in the email address or domain you’d like to always receive email from, and then click Add to list >>.

To ensure that your account filter settings are not causing incoming email not to be delivered to your account, I suggest that you visit this link:
