I am migrating users from exchange 2016 to exchange online though the users already migrated can receive emails but cannot send via the outlook desktop app. When they log into the outlook web portal they can access their mail fine. This is the error that i get on the exchange on-prem portal, how can I solve this.

"Mailbox ******@domain.com should not have external email address," ******@domain.mail.onmicrosoft.com. You can use set-mailbox mbxld - Attributes To Clear RawExternalEmailAddress to fix the corrupted attribute.
