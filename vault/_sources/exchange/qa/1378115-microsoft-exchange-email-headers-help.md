## Question

Does anyone know what the following Microsoft Exchange Email Headers mean?

X-MS-Exchange-Organization-SenderIntelligence-P2SenderOrgDomainTenantId 

and/or

X-MS-Exchange-Organization-SenderIntelligence-P2Sender

I Googled it and Could not find a whole lot of documentation/information on it.... looks like it may be some sort of tracking... but I could be reading that wrong...  

Any Microsoft Exchange gurus out there?

## Answer (community) — Microsoft Moderator

*upvotes: 0 · updated: 2023-10-02*

Hi @CyberStudent  

This header means the from address in this message is checked by Exchange Online Protection service for anti-spam and anti-phishing purpose.
