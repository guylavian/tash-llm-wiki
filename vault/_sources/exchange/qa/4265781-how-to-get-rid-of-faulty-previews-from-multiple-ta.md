## Question

I have a 2-display setup, which I use 99,9% of time. I.e., almost all the time. Laptop's display is the main one, and external Dell's 24" display (connected via HDMI) is the secondary one (prolongation of the space of the main one).

So, I am having 2 windows of the Chrome: one with its tabs on the main display, and the 2nd one on the external display. Therefore, when I hover the Chrome icon on the taskbar, it shows TWO previews: of the 1st window and of the 2nd window.

I've been using Win+<digit> hotkey for many years in a row, and there has been no problems at all with it on Win 10. But when I got updated to Windows 11, instantly a very obnoxious bug started to appear:
