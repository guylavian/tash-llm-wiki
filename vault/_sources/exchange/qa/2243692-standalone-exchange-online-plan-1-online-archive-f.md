# Standalone "Exchange Online Plan 1" Online Archive Features

## Question

Is the standalone "Exchange Online Plan 1" subscription inclusive of any version of the online archive feature? If it is included, what is the size limit? Additionally, once the online archive is enabled, does it support the application of retention policies, and are emails automatically sent to the archive as part of the default policy?

## Answer (community) — Volunteer Moderator [Mvp]

*upvotes: 1 · updated: 2025-04-03*

You need an archiving license with a P1:

https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/office365/servicedescriptions/exchange-online-service-description/exchange-online-limits

It does not archive automatically once enabled.  

If you look at this chart, you can see that its built in for Exchange policies with a P1 https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/office365/servicedescriptions/exchange-online-service-description/exchange-online-service-description#features-available-to-some-plans
