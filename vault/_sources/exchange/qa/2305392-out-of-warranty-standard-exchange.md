## Question

The keyboard on my Surface Book 3 stopped working and is no longer under warranty, so I need to pay for an Out of Warranty Standard Exchange.

When I go to my Microsoft account and start the process, it states the following: 

"The original device may be replaced with either the same model or, if unavailable, the model nearest to the original models form, functionality, and performance."

Microsoft discontinued this product.  Will Microsoft attempt to give me some other type or model of laptop, or do they have inventory of refurbished Surface Book 3s?  I just want to make sure that I will receive a refurbished Surface Book 3 before paying the $599 fee.

## Answer (community) — community member

*upvotes: 0 · updated: 2023-10-09*

Hi Travis A2,

Thank you for reaching Microsoft Community. We appreciate for sharing your concern to us. 

I am aware of your wish to learn more about the replacement procedure and the availability of Surface Book refurbished devices before proceeding with out-of-warranty services. 

As per the information provided on the Microsoft website, if the original device is no longer available, Microsoft will replace it with the model nearest to the original model's form, functionality, and performance. However, Microsoft does have inventory of refurbished Surface Book 3s, and they will attempt to provide you with a refurbished Surface Book 3 as a replacement. I recommend that you contact our live support team t to confirm the availability of refurbished Surface Book 3s before proceeding with the Out of Warranty Standard Exchange process. You may reach them by following the steps below. 

-  Go to Contact Us - Microsoft Support

-  Choose how you would like to get support for your Surface device.

-  Type in the problem you need help with.

-  Look at any available solutions offered and select Contact Support.

Any replacement process is beyond our support boundaries here in the community. And it's for this reason that we're directing you to our live support team as they are more knowledgeable for the process.
