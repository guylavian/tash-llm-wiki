I noticed that your Surface Go is experiencing the loss of hinge screws, and I really hope to directly provide you with the screw size, but after I checked all the information that I can query, unfortunately, there is no place to mark the screw size. 

While the problem may seem as simple as finding an available screw and fitting it in, it can lead to broken hinges if the wrong size is involved or if the tool is used incorrectly. So in fact, I recommend that you send your Surface to the Surface support team as soon as possible, who have the right tools to install the right size screws correctly.
