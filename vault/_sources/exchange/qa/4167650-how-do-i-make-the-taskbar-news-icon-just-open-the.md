## Question

I want to see weather in my taskbar, so i enabled the news feature, which gives a nice little temperature thing on my taskbar.

Unfortunately when I click on the icon to learn more about the current weather, I'm bombarded by a wall of garbage clickbait news articles from MSN, which i don't want to see ever.

I went to the "manage interests" settings and unchecked every single interest, but unfortunately I still see a bunch of useless garbage when i click the button.

Is there any way to fix this, or perhaps a 3rd party software that isn't as spammy as microsoft products?

That's how it's designed, unfortunately, it's going to open the news and interest. And does not really remove the interests you want as well, you can hide it but there are other stories that would show up. 

Tried searching for 3rd party weather apps in github, there's one, but it runs on browsers and does not have the capability to be added to the taskbar. The only thing I could think of that you can open that would only view weather info is the "Weather" app itself. You should be able to find that if you search for it in your start menu.
