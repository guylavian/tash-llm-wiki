Most inquiries and issues are best addressed by the development team who manages the MSN experience. Customers can submit feedback directly to the MSN team as long as the customer is using Edge. The process to do so is below:

-  Open the page in question (or MSN main page if it is not about a specific article) using the Microsoft Edge browser

-  Click on the … icon in the upper right corner to expand the menu

-  Click on Help and Feedback near the bottom

-  Click on Send Feedback

-  Note that if a customer is on a Windows machine, they can navigate directly to the Send Feedback option by pressing ALT-SHIFT-I (the letter i, not number one)

-  Be specific and concise in the feedback to get the best results

-  Advise the customer to include a valid email address

-  If appropriate to the issue being reported, include a screenshot using the option in the feedback tool
