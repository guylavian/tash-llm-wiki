Hi @ Emmanuel Agofure ,

Just want to confirm that you are following these steps to change the language setting of OWA for Exchange  online:

1.Open OWA and tap the gear icon in the upper right corner.

2.Click "常规" on the pop-up interface.

3.Select "语言" under the "语言和时间" interface.

4.and checked”重命名默认文件夹”.

5.Click Save and refresh the interface.

If the above steps still cannot change the display language of OWA, I recommend that you check the current browser language and the system language settings of the computer separately.
