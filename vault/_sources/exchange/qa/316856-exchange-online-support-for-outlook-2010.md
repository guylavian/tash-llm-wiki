## Question

I cannot find any Microsoft documentation that clearly states that Exchange Online supports Outlook 2010 even though some threads indicates that some are doing this. IS there any URL showing Microsoft's official stance on this matter? Other asked the same question and the answer came back referring to Exchange 2016 and Exchange online - but 2010 nowhere specifically mentioned.  

Please advise.

## Answer (community) — community member

*upvotes: 0 · updated: 2021-03-16*

Outlook 2010 will no longer be able to connect to Exchange Online, so your emails within the Outlook 2010 app will stop working. (Although this particular change has been delayed until 2021).
