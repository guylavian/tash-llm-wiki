## Question

In know one of the requirements to use Direct Send is that you need a licensed mailbox. Can you use a shared mailbox and enabled the associated account so you do not have to use a license?

## Answer (community) — Volunteer Moderator [Mvp]

*upvotes: 1 · updated: 2023-04-28*

No, that's against the license agreement/terms of usage. Any form of direct login to a shared mailbox is not allowed. You can however use this method with Send As permissions granted to a user with licensed mailbox.
