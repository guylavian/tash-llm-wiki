Keeping Bing as your default search engine provides an enhanced search experience in the new Microsoft Edge , including direct links to Windows apps, relevant suggestions from your organization if you’re signed in with a work or school account, and instant answers to questions about Windows. However, you can change the default search engine to any site that uses OpenSearch technology.

Edge does not change the default search engine with every update.

Edge respects user's choice and if your default search engine changes, it may be due to other reasons. For example, you may have some third-party security software that may block or undo your changes.

Change your default search engine in Microsoft Edge - Microsoft Support

Edge MSN ads are some recommended content or ads that appear on the new tab page or blank page of Edge browser. These content or ads may come from Microsoft or third-party partners. 

There are two ways to try to turn off Edge MSN ads: 

One is to block pop-ups and redirects in Edge settings. This will prevent the browser from showing unwanted pop-ups or redirects from certain sites. To block pop-ups and redirects in Edge settings, you can follow these steps: 

-  Open Edge

-  Click on the three dots (…) at the upper-right part and click on Settings

-  Click on Site permissions and click on Pop-ups and redirects

-  Make sure Block is enabled and add the site that shows ads to the block list

Another is to turn off content on the new tab page. This will turn off content, including MSN news feed and ads selected by MSN. To turn off content on the new tab page, you can follow these steps: 

-  Open a new tab in Edge

-  Click on the Page Settings gear ⚙ at the upper-right part

-  Select Custom and under Content, select Content off
