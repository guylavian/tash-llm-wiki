Change what you see when Edge opens or on the New Tab page... 

* Home page... Edge > Settings > Appearance > Home button > Set button URL . enter the webpage of your choice.  

* Start page...  Edge > Settings > Start, home and new tabs > When Edge starts > make your selection. 

*  New tab page...  Also in Edge > Settings > Start, home and new tabs > New tab page > make your selection. To have the New tab page display the webpage of your choosing, go to https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/Microsoft-Edge-Extensions-Home?hl=en-US and search for new tab

Also... You can turn off notifications for the News App.
