## Question

From Edge and initial "news" page: clicking on any article link takes an exorbitant time to load. Every link! Please advise. All systems on PC are functioning properly.

Note: All articles (with images and corresponding links) initially appear properly. Going directly to website for article, loads instantly.

I’m sorry to hear that you are experiencing slow loading of news articles on Edge.

You can try some of these steps:

> Clear your browsing history, cookies and cached data on Edge.

> Reset your Edge settings to default.

> Disable or remove any unwanted or suspicious extensions on Edge.

> Run a System File Checker (SFC) scan to check for any file corruption.

> Scan your PC for malware using Windows Defender or a trusted third-party antivirus software.

If none of these steps work, you can also try reinstalling Edge.
