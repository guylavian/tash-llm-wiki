## Question

My phone device is an iPhone.  The phone is not my primary internet device.  I use it some when traveling, waiting in waiting rooms, if I wake up at night, etc.  I used to enjoy reading the consolidated news articles on MSN.com, but no longer.  It has become an exercise in frustration.  Most times the articles are presented with a headline graphic of some sort then a few lines of the article.  After the few lines there is a blue bar saying "Continue reading on the app."  Sometimes there is an option to continue to the article [without switching to the app]; but that option occurs unpredictably and increasingly seldom.  I really would not mind the process that pushes me to use the app, if it worked in any rational way.  I have downloaded the app; so it is available on my iPhone.  If I push the blue bar saying "continue reading on the app", I am required to download it again, and I totally lose sight of the article I was wanting to read.  So, I may as well not have tried the app in the first place.  Because of this frustrating behavior, I am accessing the MSN.com News Consolidation site less and less frequently.  Surely this is not the way the process is supposed to work.  Any guidance or suggestions on what I am doing wrong?  Thanks

I really would not mind the process that pushes me to use the app, if it worked in any rational way. I have downloaded the app; so it is available on my iPhone.
