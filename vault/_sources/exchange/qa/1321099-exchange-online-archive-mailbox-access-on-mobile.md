## Answer (community) — community member [MicrosoftVendor]

*upvotes: 3 · updated: 2023-06-30*

Hi @ John Sheehan ,

According to the documentation provided by Andy, we can learn that the mobile device cannot view archive mailbox of Exchange Online is by design.

And in my tests, opening the Exchange Online mailbox using Outlook on the web and Outlook app in my phone also did not show up the archive mailbox.

Therefore, we recommend that you view the archive mailbox in an Outlook client or Outlook on Web on your computer.
