It sounds like you're experiencing significant challenges with the Active Directory Intune Connector and device enrollment issues. Here are some steps you can take to troubleshoot and seek support:

-  Diagnostics: In the Microsoft Intune admin center, you can run diagnostics to identify issues related to your account. This may help pinpoint the cause of the certificate error and device enrollment failures.

-  Contact Support: Since you've already submitted tickets, ensure that you include as much detail as possible in your support requests. This includes timestamps, device IDs, and any error messages you encounter. You can also use the Contact Support option in the Intune admin center to file an online support ticket if you haven't done so already.

-  Review Support Options: Make sure to check the support options available in the Intune admin center. You can find various resources that may assist you in resolving your issues.

-  Service Requests: Keep track of your service requests in the Intune admin center to monitor the status of your tickets and any responses from Microsoft support.

If the problem persists, consider escalating your issue through the support channels you have already used, as they may provide more immediate assistance given the urgency of your situation.
