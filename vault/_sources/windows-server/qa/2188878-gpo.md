## Question

Hi All,

I am involved in a server hardening project using a GPO as the hardening tool.

We have numerous service accounts where the password cannot be changed due to the nature of the applications they support. I have noticed that one of the GPO policies asks users to change passwords every 30 days. I believe this will also force the service accounts to change their passwords also. 

Can I amend this policy to not include the service accounts allowing them to bypass the password change policy. I know that we have Item Level Targetting and wondered if this would suffice, or is there a better way of bypassing the service accounts?

Any help or advice would be greatly appreciated.
