# `truststores`

**The `spec.truststores` field on the [[keycloak-cr]] — supplies trusted CA
certificates to the RHBK server from Kubernetes Secrets.**

```yaml
spec:
  truststores:
    my-truststore:
      secret:
        name: my-secret
```

- Secrets may hold **PEM** files or **PKCS12** files (`.p12` / `.pfx`).
- The referenced Secret must be in the **same namespace** as the CR.
- On Kubernetes/OpenShift the well-known cluster CA bundles are included
  automatically when present:
  `/var/run/secrets/kubernetes.io/serviceaccount/ca.crt` and `service-ca.crt`.

## Why it matters with custom images

The Operator is unaware of TLS config baked into a [[custom-keycloak-image]]. If
the image serves the management interface over `https`, set both `tlsSecret` and
`truststores` on the CR so the Operator probes the interface over the right scheme.

## Contradictions / caveats

- Consistent across 26.0–26.6.
