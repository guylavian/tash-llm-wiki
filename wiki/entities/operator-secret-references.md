# Secret & ConfigMap references

**How the [[keycloak-cr]] points at Kubernetes Secrets/ConfigMaps, and how the
Operator reacts when their contents change.**

- Secret references are used by dedicated fields (e.g. `tlsSecret`) and as values
  in [[additional-options]]. ConfigMap references back options like `configMapFile`.
- The referenced Secret/ConfigMap **must be in the same namespace** as the CR.
- The Operator **polls approximately every minute** for changes to referenced
  Secrets/ConfigMaps. On a meaningful change it performs a **rolling restart** of
  the RHBK Deployment to pick up the new content.

## Contradictions / caveats

- Consistent across 26.0–26.6. The ~1-minute poll means Secret rotation is not
  instantaneous; expect up to a minute plus the restart.
