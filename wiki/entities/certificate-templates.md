# Certificate templates (AD CS)

**The sets of rules and settings a CA applies to incoming certificate requests —
they preconfigure a certificate for a task and tell the client how to build a valid
request.**

## Body

- **Only an Enterprise CA can issue from a template.** Standalone CAs have no
  templates, so the request itself must carry all certificate details.
- Templates are stored in **AD DS**, so **every CA in the forest** sees the current
  standard template — this is what keeps certificate policy consistent forest-wide.
- Managed with the **Certificate Templates snap-in**: view properties, copy and
  modify, and control which users/computers can **read** the template and **enroll**
  for it (the read/enroll ACL is what gates autoenrollment). Managing templates needs
  **Domain Admins** (or equivalent).
- Templates are the enforcement point for higher-assurance issuance — e.g. **TPM key
  attestation** (require the key be TPM-protected; RSA-only, Enterprise-CA-only) is a
  template setting.

Because the read/enroll permissions on a template directly authorize who can obtain
that certificate type, templates are a sensitive object class worth auditing
alongside other privileged AD delegations (inferred — consistent with the
least-privilege guidance in [[securing-active-directory]]).
