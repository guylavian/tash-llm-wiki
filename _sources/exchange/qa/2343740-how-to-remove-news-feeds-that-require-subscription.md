Hey CoolGrammie!

I know of many news websites that are covered in advertisements, and that require you to subscribe to them to continue reading. But that is the news provider's fault, not Bing's. Bing itself is completely free, and has no advertisements on the page.

Bing is just a search engine that scans the internet for News Articles, and places them in a News tab. Bing does not create any news at all.

Nonetheless, I do understand your frustration with ads. There are two main Microsoft solutions to that:

*You could start using Reading View in Microsoft Edge.*Reading View doesn't work on all of the news sites, but when it does, it is worth it. It clears away all of the ads and toolbars, keeping only the text, headings, and pictures. To get Reading
 View in Microsoft Edge (no, Chrome doesn't have it):

-  You can click the Reading View button on the URL bar (or press Ctrl+Shift+R)

-  You can put "read:" in front of the URL of your news site.

Remember, this won't prevent site from demanding a subscription, but it will clear away the ads.

*You could download the Microsoft News app.*I personally use the Microsoft News app as my primary news source. It is absolutely free, and the news articles are beautifully formatted to include no ads.
To download the Microsoft news app:

-  Open the Microsoft Store, search for Microsoft News, and select "Get".

Remember, this won't give you points for using it, but it is free of charge and ads.

I hope that this solved your problem! Upvote me and mark me as helpful if it did! (And if it didn't, well, I'm sorry. 😊)
