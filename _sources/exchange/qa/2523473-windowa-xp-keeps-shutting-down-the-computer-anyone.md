Hi KarlOakes,

·        
Have you set any power plan on the computer?

·        
Is the issue confined while performing specific task or launching any program?

Follow the suggestions listed below for a possible fix:

Method 1: You may check if Automatic restart is enabled. To do the same follow the steps:

a.      
Click the Start button, right-click
My Computer, click Properties, click the advanced tab, and then click Settings under Startup and Recovery.

b.     
Under System Failure

c.      
Uncheck the option
‘Automatically restart’

**Method 2:**To narrow down on the issue, I would suggest you to check the behavior by booting the computer in clean boot state. You may refer the article and check.

How to configure Windows XP to start in a "clean boot" state

http://support.microsoft.com/kb/310353

If the issue does not persist you may continue with step 2 from the above link.

Make sure you reset the computer to normal mode after the troubleshooting completes. Refer the section “Steps to configure Windows to use a Normal startup state”

Let us know if this helps.
