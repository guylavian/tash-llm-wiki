## Question

We are facing Mobile sync issue. after configuring Mobile outlook mail sync only for 30 days. is it default feature or its issue.

## Answer (community) — Microsoft Moderator

*upvotes: 0 · updated: 2023-11-07*

Hi @Md. Mahfuzur Rahman,

Welcome to our Q&A forum!

Based on my research, the mobile app is only capable of loading up to 1000 messages and/or one month’s worth of messages. This is in place so that the app doesn’t take up a lot of storage on your device. However, you may be able to search for older emails by tapping “Load More” once you reach the bottom of the page.

If you would like to suggest a feature for a future update, you can do so by going to Outlook app Settings > Help & Feedback > Suggest a Feature.
