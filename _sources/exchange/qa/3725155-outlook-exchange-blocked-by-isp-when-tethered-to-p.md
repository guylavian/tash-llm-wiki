## Question

Well, apparently my cell phone provider partially blocks access to Microsoft Exchange when you are accessing the internet while tethered to your phone. I would be curious to hear of other people’s experience with this.  Here is the scenario.

Running Windows 10 and Outlook 2016. Configured a Hotmail account to use Outlook 2016. Works flawlessly when on home internet, whether hard-wired or wireless. Works flawlessly when on another home internet connection at a neighbor’s house.  So now, on
 the same machine I connect to the internet via my cell phone’s wireless hotspot feature. The SSID appears on the computer and I select that to connect. No other internet connection is present. What happens is that Outlook keeps asking me for my password over
 and over again.  Obviously I put in the right password but it just keeps prompting me. It will not connect to Hotmail. Now, with that same connection, I connect to a VPN service that I subscribe to. Just to be on the safe side, I do a “ipconfig /flush dns”. 
 Outlook does prompt me once, but when I put in the right password, it connects to Hotmail just fine.  Reboot computer. Connect to the internet NOT using the tethering feature of the phone.  Outlook connects to Hotmail just fine.  I try tethering to my phone
 and disabling all other connections and get the same prompting for password over and over again and it never connects to Hotmail.  Again, if I connect to my VPN service I have to put in the password ONE TIME and then it works fine after that. It is totally
 repeatable.  I have done nothing with Outlook profiles on the machine. Same profile. Also I have done nothing with any settings in the Mail Profile or in Outlook.  Everything is consistent there. Apparently the wireless (phone) provider does not allow connection
 directly to Exchange with you are using tethering, unless you are using VPN over tethering which is different.  Any comments or insights on this and have you seen it with your cell phone provider?
