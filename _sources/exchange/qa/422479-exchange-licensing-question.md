## Question

Hi,  

My organisation are considering moving over to Microsoft Office 365 Standard Business but we have users which have multiple emails with different domains. For example:  

******@hostname1.com  

******@hostname2.com  

******@hostname3.com  

Just wondering if we can have this under one licence or if multiple licences are required?  

Thanks in advanced

## Answer (community) — community member

*upvotes: 1 · updated: 2021-06-07*

@Chris      

As AndyDavid said, the license is based on account rather than email address. One account could has multiple email address.    

If you want to use those email address suffix in Office 365, you need to verify them in Office 365 admin center first: Add a domain to Microsoft 365
