## Question

I use Mac Mail to compose, send and receive email. I pay monthly for Microsoft Exchange server to host my email accounts. That's about the limit of my email knowledge! I was told to post here because this forum "is designed to support advanced users like yourself." I'm not an advanced user so please, dumb any answers down for me!

I bought a new Mac Studio. I used the Mac Mail "Add Account" dialog box that clearly offers Microsoft Exchange as one of the options. It asks me for my email address. I give it. Then it asks me for a passworrd. For some reason the password I entered doesn't seem to work. No matter. They send me a "One-Time Use" password and I get into my Microsoft account. I'm able to add the mail account. But the Inbox shows up empty.

I have this Exchange email account on four other Apple devices including a MacBook laptop, two iPads and my iPhone. I can get and send email on all my devices no problem ... except the new Mac Studio. 

This has added to my confusion. Mac Mail says the Exchange account is online. I can send email from the account. I just can't receive any! 

I keep coming back to the fact that when I tried to use the password it was incorrect. Just to check it, I went to outlook.office.com where I've gone before to pick up mail. Sure enough, when I try to sign in again it says my password is wrong. But again, it offers my the one-time code to bypass the password. When I use it, I can indeed get into my box. It shows I'm signed in. I see my picture in the corner and I can access my account information but, oddly, even on the web my inbox is empty! Again, I can see my Exchange email on four other devices. The box is definitely not empty!

This has taken my confusion and stirred in some fear. I tried Microsoft Support. The guy told me that Outlook no longer supports third party mail accounts. I told him that I can use my Exchange accounts (my wife and I both have them) on all my devices. I also told him that Apple Mail offers Microsoft Exchange as an option. He insists no, that soon I won't be able to use Exchange on my Macs at all. He's fightened me! I'm reluctant to change my password. I'm afraid that if I change it, I might not be able to access my email anywhere!!!

Any thoughts? I'm kinda desparate!!
