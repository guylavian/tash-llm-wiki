Hi @cscowx  ,

Why does Outlook client on mobile not find the email recall button? How does Outlook client on mobile support email retraction?

It's actually the expected behavior that there's no recall button in Outlook for mobile. Because the message recall feature is currently only available in Outlook for Windows.
