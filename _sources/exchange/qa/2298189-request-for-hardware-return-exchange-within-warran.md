## Answer (community) — community member

*upvotes: 0 · updated: 2023-05-29*

Hi Svetozar Ruzicka, 

thank you for reaching Microsoft Community and sorry to hear about the issue that you experience on your Surface Laptop 4 especially with the dead pixel issue. We understand that the device is not cheap, and you should not be having this issue on a new device. 

Dead pixel issue is a hardware issue already in which no troubleshooting could fix and the device would need to be serviced. However, for any service concerns or assistance in creating a service order for your Surface, please contact our live support instead for further assistance as we do not have the needed tools here in the Community and so this is already beyond our support boundaries. You may reach our live support through these steps below: 

-  Go to Contact Us - Microsoft Support

-  Choose how you would like to get support for your Surface device.

-  Type in the problem you need help with.

-  Look at any available solutions offered and select Contact Support.

You may also check this forum article by Barb Bowman on getting chat or phone support for other details: How to get Phone and Chat Support (for Surface Home/Consumer - Microsoft Community

Hope this helps.
